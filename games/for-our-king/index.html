The idea

One keep. Everything else is coming for it.

For Our King is a single player Roguelite Tower Defence and Autobattler built on one idea. Defend the keep against waves of enemies, recruit different unit types, and cast spells from different Schools of Magic.

Progression

Declare Your Royal Decrees

Earn Gems, Essence, and Crowns to unlock over 300 different nodes to strengthen your Keep, Units, Economy, and cast spells.

The Royal Court

Assemble Your Royal Court

Add flat and mult modifiers, create broken synergies, and stack unique effects until you break the game beyond its limits.

Build a bar

Six slots, ten decrees. Drag them in. They resolve left to right, so the order you choose is the whole thing.

Endgame

Ascend Through Prestige

Some doors never open through honest service. Spend Essence to prestige and raze your entire tree in order to take one of six permanently sealed unlocks in exchange, then climb again but stronger than you were before.
