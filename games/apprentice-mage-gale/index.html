The party

The Party's Only Source of Damage

Play as Gale, the Apprentice Mage, traveling with a party of adventurers on a journey to defeat the Demon King. Unlike the heroes beside you, Gale is the party's ONLY source of damage.

Companions

Build Your Team

Each of your companions has their own class, roles, and subclasses. Upgrade their skills, enhance their talents, customize their role, and find your own strategy with them.

Spells

Become a Powerful Mage

Master over 20 unique spells, each with its own strengths and uses. Set foes burning with Fireball, then Combust them. Freeze them solid with Deep Freeze, then Shatter the ice.

Choose carefully which spells to bring and discover combinations that can turn an ordinary apprentice into a formidable mage.

Skill tree

Build Your Own Path

Explore a massive skill tree with 300+ nodes. Strengthen Gale, enhance your party, choose subclasses, and shape your build around the spells and strategies you discover.

Eight lands

Journey Beyond the Familiar

Fight your way through 20+ dungeons across eight lands: the Meadow, the Thornwood, the Bone Dunes, the Frozen Pass, the Deep, the Ashen Peaks, the Hollow City, and the Starfall.

Each land has its own monsters and its own boss, from the Orc Warlord to the Light Valkyrie, and the Ledger records every enemy you meet.

The journey may be long.

But perhaps, someday, you'll look back and realize just how far this little apprentice has come.
